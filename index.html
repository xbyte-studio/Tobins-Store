What is an Orient inverter bulb?

It is an LED bulb with a rechargeable battery built inside. It lights your home like any normal bulb, and when the power goes out it keeps glowing on its own battery for up to 4 hours. No separate inverter wiring is needed.

Power is on

The bulb works as a regular LED and charges its battery in the background.

Power cut

It switches to battery automatically. There is no switch to press and no dark gap.

Power returns

It goes back to mains and recharges, ready for the next cut.

Why a standing wheelchair

The NeoStand by NeoMotion lets its user rise from sitting to standing at the push of a joystick. Standing supports therapy, health and independence: reaching a shelf, talking face to face, or simply seeing the world from a new height.
